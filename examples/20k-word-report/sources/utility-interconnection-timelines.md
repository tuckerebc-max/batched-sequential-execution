# Utility-Reported Queue Data, Selected Utilities (fictional)

Data pulled from public interconnection dashboards maintained by three
investor-owned utilities and one municipal utility, Q4 2025 filings.

| Utility | Residential BESS median (days) | Commercial BESS median (days) | Active queue size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verdant Power & Light | 44 | 132 | 3,102 |
| Highline Electric Cooperative | 71 | 165 | 640 |
| Basin Public Utility District | 58 | 140 | 1,215 |
| Norhaven Energy | 96 | 210 | 4,780 |

Norhaven Energy's queue grew 34% between Q4 2024 and Q4 2025, which Norhaven
attributes in its own filing to a surge in commercial BESS applications tied
to demand-charge management rather than to any change in its review process.

All four utilities report that supplemental review — triggered when an
application requires a distribution system impact study — adds a median of
90 additional days regardless of system size, and that supplemental review
is the single largest driver of queue time variance across applications.
