# CHARTER — The Last Utility, Feedback Sweep

## Deliverable
What: Resolution of all reviewer feedback (developmental edit, beta reads,
fact-check, copyedit) on the ~30,000-word manuscript "The Last Utility: How
Municipal Broadband Became America's New Public Works Fight" (preface, nine
chapters, back matter). Each item is either applied or explicitly ruled out
with a one-line reason; the manuscript's approximate length is preserved.
Format: a per-unit resolution log (markdown), one entry per feedback item,
plus any inline chapter-text edit the item requires. This run's "total
size" below counts feedback items, not manuscript words — the manuscript's
current length is documented per chapter in sources/manuscript-ch1-9-excerpt.md
and is not itself the unit of work.
Total size: 120items

## Through-line
Every one of the 120 reviewer items is either applied to the manuscript or explicitly ruled out
with a stated reason, and the manuscript that emerges still argues its original thesis at its
original length.

## Audience
The author and the developmental editor, reviewing the resolution log
before the manuscript goes to copyedit proper. They need to see, for every
one of the 120 logged items, what happened to it and why.

## Voice & register
Not applicable to the resolution log itself (a log, not prose); any
manuscript text touched while resolving an item must match that chapter's
existing register — a trade-nonfiction narrative voice, not a policy-report
voice. Do not "improve" surrounding prose beyond what the feedback item
itself asks for.

## Non-negotiables
1. Every one of the 120 items logged in sources/reviewer-feedback-log.md is
   either applied or explicitly ruled out with a one-line reason; none may
   be silently dropped.
2. Every named, on-the-record interview subject keeps their full name and
   title on first reference in their chapter — pseudonyms or anonymization
   are not used for a named, on-the-record source without a recorded
   RULING (accountability-journalism standard).
3. The terminology lock is used exactly; no synonyms substituted mid-sweep.
4. No chapter's word count changes by more than 10% from its current
   length (documented in sources/manuscript-ch1-9-excerpt.md) as a side
   effect of applying feedback.
5. Structural feedback (reordering sections, cutting subsections) is
   applied only where an item explicitly requests it; do not "improve"
   anything unprompted.

## Terminology lock
- co-op -> cooperative
- state house -> statehouse
- rate-setting -> rate design
- match funding -> matching funds
- town hall -> public meeting

## Sources of truth
- [S1] sources/manuscript-ch1-9-excerpt.md — chapter structure, current
  per-chapter word counts, and representative excerpts
- [S2] sources/reviewer-feedback-log.md — all 120 numbered reviewer
  comments, in manuscript order, tagged by chapter/section region

## Acceptance criteria
1. All 120 items in sources/reviewer-feedback-log.md show a resolution
   (applied or ruled-out-with-reason) in the final combined resolution log.
2. Zero occurrences of a forbidden terminology form remain in any unit's
   resolution log.
3. No named, on-the-record source is anonymized or pseudonymized without a
   recorded RULING.
4. Each unit's chapter-region word-count delta (where the unit reports one)
   is within 10% of that chapter's pre-revision length.
5. The final reconciliation unit confirms no two units' resolutions
   contradict each other (e.g., different chapters restating the same
   figure differently after revision).

## Out of scope
- Reordering chapters or changing the book's overall argument.
- Adding new chapters or case studies not already in the manuscript.
- Any copyedit-level correction not raised by a logged feedback item.
