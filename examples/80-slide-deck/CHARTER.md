# CHARTER — Meridian Grid Robotics Q3 2026 Investor Update

## Deliverable
What: A full edit pass on the existing 80-slide Voltane Robotics investor
deck, applying the post-merger rebrand (new company name, ticker, product
name, and brand colors) and refreshing every financial figure to Q3 2026
actuals, ahead of the Q3 2026 earnings call.
Format: markdown edit log per slide range (this run never touches the real
.pptx); the format skill applies the approved edits to the actual file
afterward.
Total size: 80slides

## Through-line
Every slide in the deck presents Meridian Grid Robotics as a single post-merger company with
Q3 2026 actuals throughout — no residual Voltane naming, no stale figures, and no slide whose
numbers contradict another's.

## Audience
Existing and prospective institutional investors and sell-side analysts on
the Q3 2026 earnings call, plus the IR team rehearsing from this deck. They
have seen the pre-merger deck before and will notice any inconsistency
between slides.

## Voice & register
This is an edit pass, not composition — no new narrative voice is being
authored. Slide text that is not explicitly targeted by a non-negotiable
below is left as-is; the register of any new or replaced text matches the
existing deck's register (concise, declarative, investor-deck norms).

## Non-negotiables
1. Every on-screen occurrence of "Voltane Robotics" becomes "Meridian Grid
   Robotics"; every occurrence of the ticker "VOLT" becomes "MRDN"; every
   occurrence of "battery pack" (as the product name) becomes "energy
   module." Presenter-only speaker notes are out of scope for this pass.
2. Every financial figure on an investor-facing slide is replaced with its
   Q3 2026 actual from sources/q3-2026-actuals.md and carries an [S#] tag in
   this edit log; a figure not present in that source is left unchanged and
   flagged, not guessed at.
3. Chart and divider colors move from the old palette (#2F7A3C green family)
   to the new palette (#1B3A5C slate blue primary, #E8A33D amber accent).
4. The deck's slide count remains exactly 80; no slide is added or removed
   in this pass.
5. A quarter-over-quarter comparison is always labeled "Q3 2026 vs. Q3 2025"
   exactly, per the earnings-call script.

## Terminology lock
- Voltane Robotics -> Meridian Grid Robotics
- Voltane -> Meridian Grid Robotics
- VOLT -> MRDN
- battery pack -> energy module
- battery packs -> energy modules

## Sources of truth
- [S1] sources/rebrand-styleguide.md — naming, color, and figure-sourcing rules
- [S2] sources/q3-2026-actuals.md — approved Q3 2026 financial figures
- [S3] sources/original-deck-outline.md — the existing 80-slide deck content

## Acceptance criteria
1. Zero occurrences of "Voltane" or "VOLT" remain anywhere in the edited
   deck content (speaker notes excepted).
2. Every figure named in sources/q3-2026-actuals.md that appeared in the
   original deck is updated and [S2]-tagged in the edit log.
3. The reconciliation pass confirms slide count is unchanged at 80 and
   finds no wave that duplicated or skipped a slide number.
4. No two waves independently invent different replacement text for the
   same recurring element (e.g. the footer wordmark, the TOC section names).
5. Every chart-color reference in the edit log names the new palette hex
   value, not a description like "the new blue."

## Out of scope
- Redesigning slide layouts or information architecture.
- Editing presenter-only speaker notes.
- Any content in the appendix beyond the specific figures and terminology
  named above (e.g. no rewriting of risk-factor legal language).
- Producing the final .pptx file — batches here are the edit log only.
