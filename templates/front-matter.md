# Bridging the Interconnection Queue

State energy offices face a decision window that will not stay open:
battery storage capacity is arriving faster than most interconnection queues
were designed to process, and the states that update their rules first will
absorb that capacity at a fraction of the cost of the states that wait.

## Executive summary

This report surveys current interconnection queue policy across states,
documents where FERC Order 2222-B's implementation is furthest along, and
lays out three model-legislation options with their cost, timeline, and
stakeholder tradeoffs made explicit rather than implied.
