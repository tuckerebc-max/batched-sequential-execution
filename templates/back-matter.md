## Conclusion

The states furthest along in adapting their interconnection queues to
distributed storage share one trait: they treated cost allocation and
technical standards as a single decision, not two. Of the three model-
legislation options in Section 5.1, the report's own case studies (4.1, 4.2)
favor the shared-queue-priority approach on cost grounds and the hosting-
capacity-map approach on implementation speed — the tradeoff a legislature
adopting either option should expect to make explicitly, not discover later.
